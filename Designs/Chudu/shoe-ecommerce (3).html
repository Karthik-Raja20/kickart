About SOLEVAULT

SoleVault was born out of frustration — the frustration of spending hours across multiple apps comparing prices just to find the best deal on a pair of shoes. We built a smarter solution.

We aggregate live prices from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio and more so you never have to. Our platform is built for the smart shopper who wants quality footwear without the premium price tag.

We partner only with authentic, verified brands including Nike, Adidas, Puma, Converse, Reebok and more — ensuring every pair you receive is 100% genuine.
