Frequently Asked Questions
How does the price comparison work?

Our engine checks prices across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and Ajio every 15 minutes. When you view a product, the table shows live prices from all 4 platforms so you can always buy at the lowest available price.

Are all products 100% authentic?

Yes, absolutely. We partner exclusively with brand-authorised distributors and have a strict zero-counterfeit policy. Every product comes with original brand packaging and a certificate of authenticity.

What is the return and exchange policy?

You can return or exchange any product within 30 days of delivery. Returns are completely free — we arrange doorstep pickup. No questions asked, full refund processed within 3-5 business days.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2-4 business days across India. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available in 500+ cities. You'll receive a tracking link as soon as your order is dispatched.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, EMI (0% for 3 months), wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, GPay), and Cash on Delivery for orders under ₹10,000.
